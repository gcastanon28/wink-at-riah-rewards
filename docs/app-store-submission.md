## Description

Wink At Riah Rewards is the private loyalty app for Wink At Riah lash clients.

Clients can sign up, view their current point balance, track reward redemption history, browse available lash perks, and redeem points for eligible rewards. The app also includes profile management, communication preferences, appointment booking links, referral sharing, password reset, and in-app account deletion.

Use Wink At Riah Rewards to:

- Check your lash reward point balance
- See progress toward the next available reward
- Redeem rewards for future visits
- Review redemption history
- Share a referral link with a friend
- Manage profile and communication preferences
- Open the Wink At Riah booking portal
- Delete your rewards account from Profile settings

Rewards have no cash value and may vary based on current Wink At Riah business rules.

## App Review Notes

Wink At Riah Rewards is an account-based loyalty app for lash clients.

Reviewer credentials:

- Username: `<create demo account email>`
- Password: `<demo account password>`

Core review flow:

1. Log in with the provided demo customer account.
2. Open Dashboard to view points and reward progress.
3. Open Rewards Catalog to view available rewards and redemption states.
4. Open My Points to view earning rules.
5. Open Visit History to view redemption history.
6. Open Profile to update profile preferences, sign out, or delete the account.
7. Referral sharing uses the native iOS share sheet when available and falls back to copying the referral link.

Staff-only point awarding exists at `/staff/points`. This is restricted to approved Wink At Riah staff emails and is not expected to be available to ordinary customer demo accounts.

No in-app purchases are offered. Rewards have no cash value and are redeemed against loyalty points managed by Wink At Riah.
